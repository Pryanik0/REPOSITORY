Home Join us Vacancies Warehouse Supervisor Location: Santa Perpètua de Mogoda, Catalonia, Spain - Category: Warehouse Supervisor Description Centrient Pharmaceuticals is the leading manufacturer of beta-lactam antibiotics, and a provider of next generation statins and anti-fungals. We produce and sell intermediates, active pharmaceutical ingredients and finished dosage forms. We stand proudly at the centre of modern healthcare, as a maker of essential and life-saving medicines. With our commitment to Quality, Reliability and Sustainability at the heart of everything we do, our over 2000 employees work continuously to meet our customers’ needs. We work towards a sustainable future by actively participating in the fight against antimicrobial resistance. Founded 150 years ago as the ‘Nederlandsche Gist- en Spiritusfabriek’, our company was known as Gist Brocades. Headquartered in Rotterdam (Netherlands), we have production facilities and sales offices in China, India, the Netherlands, Spain, the United States and Mexico. Centrient Pharmaceuticals is wholly owned by Bain Capital Private Equity, a leading global private investment firm. The Position Reporting to the Head - Supply Chain Management, the incumbent is responsible for all physical warehouse activities, inbound, storage and outbound of products and materials, and timely and accurate registration of all related warehouse movements and inventory differences in the warehouse management system. Manage, coach and develop warehouse operators, executing & overseeing strategy warehouse and materials management implementation and continuously improving spend management in the area, all this activities in full compliance with applicable SHE regulations and c-GMP requirements. The main activities and responsibilities: Comply to the company, SHE&Q and security regulations; signal unsafe situations, (near) incidents, environmental, quality and security risks; stop proceedings when serious violations of the regulations occur and report these. Address others to comply with the regulations to prevent unsafe behavior. Manage the operations of warehouse, lead the team operators (organize daily tasks, holidays, managing conflicts…), and keep in full service of operation the related facilities in accordance with the standards. Keep up to date and maintain the facilities. Propose and maintain SOP’s for warehousing and materials management (warehousing, pick and pack lead-times) complying with the legislation in force and internal regulations. Take care of proper management reporting of the KPI’s for stock and proactively managing inventory levels (raw materials, finished goods, tank farm…). Identifying and implementing improvement projects in terms of processes, service and cost related to logistics. Responsible for monitoring actual supplier lead times, supply size consignments and service levels in order to draw up the supply plan of starting references (raw materials, packaging, mro’s…). Responsible for purchase requisitions, including the call off orders to be sent to the qualified and agreed upon suppliers based on the deployed Frame-Work Agreements. Warehouse related complaints and participating in the complaint-team with Quality and supporting the Customers service and Sales in solving issues. SCADA and climate control system administrator. Support during Internal and external audits . Requirements The Ideal Candidate: Required Education Level in Logistics, Supply Chain, Engineering or a related field.ogisticor a related field. +5 years of experience in warehouse and materials management within an industrial or manufacturing environment. Previous experience supervising operational teams and coordinating daily warehouse activities. Experience managing raw materials, packaging materials, MRO supplies and finished goods. Experience in chemical, pharmaceutical or other regulated manufacturing environments will be highly valued. Experience with ERP systems, preferably SAP. Fluent Spanish and good command of English (minimum B2 level). Strong leadership, organizational, communication and problem-solving skills. Proactive mindset, with a focus on safety, continuous improvement and operational efficiency. The organization is very flat. You have a lot of freedom and responsibility in an open, direct, and informal atmosphere.
