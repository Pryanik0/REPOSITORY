We produce and sell intermediates, active pharmaceutical ingredients and finished dosage forms. We stand proudly at the centre of modern healthcare, as a maker of essential and life-saving medicines. With our commitment to Quality, Reliability and Sustainability at the heart of everything we do, our over 2000 employees work continuously to meet our customers’ needs. We work towards a sustainable future by actively participating in the fight against antimicrobial resistance. Founded 150 years ago as the ‘Nederlandsche Gist- en Spiritusfabriek’, our company was known as Gist Brocades. Headquartered in Rotterdam (Netherlands), we have production facilities and sales offices in China, India, the Netherlands, Spain, the United States and Mexico. Centrient Pharmaceuticals is wholly owned by Bain Capital Private Equity, a leading global private investment firm. The Position The Commercial / Customer Service department has a vacancy in its Barcelona team for the following position. The Customer Service Agent owns the order management process end-to-end and acts as a go-to person for customer and order related questions, both internally and externally. The Customer Service Agent will act as a SPOC for all customer related matters. He/ She will strongly liaise with related functions (Sales, Supply planning, Logistics) to drive the order management process. The Customer Service Agent reports to the Regional Customer Service Agent Team Lead. The main activities and responsibilities: Responsible for End-to-End Order Life Cycle for assigned accounts (PFI, Confirmation, Order Booking, Cleaning, Dispatch and Documentation). Dedicated real-time customer Support via phone calls, regular catch-up meetings, F2F meetings and emails. Responsible for meeting all KPI’s related to customer segmentation. Co-ordination with Sales, Supply Chain and Finance such as Planning, WH, International Logistics, export, import & Finance team for on time deliveries. Status monitoring and driving fulfillment of open orders, In transit deliveries or any schedule updates, including escalation mngt. Alignment with sales manager franchise managers on the demand allocation, deviations and resolution. Batch allocation as per customer requirements, Share all relevant documentation as per CMS. Shipment planning (rolling months) for all domestic, export and intercompany shipments including lead time considerations. Order related document handling. Proactive communication to Customers, Sales and relevant internal stakeholders. Complaint handling for price and quantity. Coordination of return process with logistics. Ownership of customer master data records. Requirements The Ideal Candidate: Minimum High School Diploma or equivalent. Minimum 5 years of experience in B2B Customer Service, Order Management or Sales Administration within an industrial or manufacturing environment. Proven experience managing the end-to-end order management cycle, from order entry and validation to delivery coordination, invoicing and after-sales follow-up. Previous experience managing international customers and orders within a European or global B2B environment is essential. Good understanding of international logistics and shipping processes, including Incoterms, delivery terms and coordination with logistics and distribution departments. Advanced English (C1 level), with proven experience using English daily in a professional environment. Hands-on experience with SAP ERP for order processing, order tracking and customer account management is required. Strong customer orientation, communication and problem-solving skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively across international and cross-functional teams. Benefits Terms and reward We offer a local contract in a challenging, comprehensive, operational environment, with many opportunities for personal input and growth and plenty of discussions with other professionals. The organization is very flat. You have a lot of freedom and responsibility in an open, direct, and informal atmosphere.
