Home Join us Vacancies Logistics Coordinator (Temporary) Location: Santa Perpètua de Mogoda, Catalonia, Spain - Category: Logistics Coordinator (Temporary) Description Centrient Pharmaceuticals is the leading manufacturer of beta-lactam antibiotics, and a provider of next generation statins and anti-fungals. We produce and sell intermediates, active pharmaceutical ingredients and finished dosage forms. We stand proudly at the centre of modern healthcare, as a maker of essential and life-saving medicines. With our commitment to Quality, Reliability and Sustainability at the heart of everything we do, our over 2200 employees work continuously to meet our customers’ needs. We work towards a sustainable future by actively participating in the fight against antimicrobial resistance. Founded 150 years ago as the ‘Nederlandsche Gist- en Spiritusfabriek’, our company was known as Gist Brocades. Headquartered in Rotterdam (Netherlands), we have production facilities and sales offices in China, India, the Netherlands, Spain, the United States and Mexico. Centrient Pharmaceuticals is wholly owned by Bain Capital Private Equity, a leading global private investment firm. The Supply Chain Management Department has a vacancy in Santa Perpètua / Sant Feliu de Llobregat offices for the following position: Logistics Coordinator (Temporary) Main activities and responsibilities: Manage, coordinate, and execute the logistic inbound and outbound flow of goods at the Spain site, in coordination with the relevant partner functions and transport suppliers. Secure service levels and excellence in execution in quality and documentation right first time, minimizing costs and errors in transport booking and fulfillment. Lead the customer complaints process, solving difficult or repetitive issues with customers, with a continuous improvement approach, through root-cause analysis and corrective actions. Partner with Customer Service and Planning team to fulfill monthly demand volumes and prioritize dispatch of goods based on business requirements and available schedules. Interact directly with internal customers and via sales department with external customers to provide the right customer support and improve the customer experience. You are an expert in International Trade & Logistics procedures, familiar with multimodal transport modes and export and import procedures. Own and report logistic KPI’s and define follow-up actions for improvement. You maintain and improve relations with logistics service suppliers, supporting in the preparation of the negotiations of present and/or current contracts and agreements. Requirements The Ideal Candidate Bachelor's degree in Logistics, Supply Chain or relevant discipline or a comparable skills and knowledge level gained by experience. At least 5 years of experience in Logistics, Demand/Supply Chain or other Supply Chain related roles. Fluent proficiency in Spanish and English language (written and spoken). Relevant experience in SAP. Extensive knowledge about Incoterms and GDP is preferred. Experience in working in an international environment. Flexibility and availability to commute to both our Sant Feliu de Llobregat and Santa Perpètua de Mogoda offices. Benefits Terms and reward We offer a temporary local contract in a challenging, comprehensive, operational environment. The organization is very flat.
