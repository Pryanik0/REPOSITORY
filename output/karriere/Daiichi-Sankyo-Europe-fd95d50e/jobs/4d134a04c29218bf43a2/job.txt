You will shape the technical and strategic direction of workforce planning, partnering with business leaders and HR globally to translate strategic workforce questions into actionable solutions and insights. This is a highly visible global role that combines advanced workforce analytics, strategic consulting, and executive stakeholder engagement to support capability, capacity and workforce investment decisions across the organization. The position can be filled at one of our European affiliates (with a preference for placement in Germany), the US or Japan.
