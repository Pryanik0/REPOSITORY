Business Transformation & Excellence Manager (m/f/x)

Bei Daiichi Sankyo stehen wir vereint hinter einem einzigen Ziel: Das Leben auf der ganzen Welt durch innovative Medikamente zu verbessern. Mit aktiver Innovation seit 1899, Präsenz in mehr als 30 Ländern und über 19.000 Mitarbeitern entwickeln wir bahnbrechende Therapien in der Onkologie, für Herz-Kreislauf-Erkrankungen, seltene Krankheiten und Immunerkrankungen. Im Einklang mit unserer Vision 2030, "ein innovatives globales Gesundheitsunternehmen zu sein, das zur nachhaltigen Entwicklung der Gesellschaft beiträgt", gestalten wir eine gesündere, hoffnungsvollere Zukunft für Patienten, ihre Familien und die Gesellschaft. Purpose of the function Daiichi Sankyo TechUnit Europe is strengthening its ability to convert strategic decisions into sustainable organizational outcomes. This role leads a focused portfolio of high-impact, time-bound transformations that cross functions, sites and global-regional-local interfaces. It combines operating-model design, implementation leadership and change adoption, then transfers stable ownership to the line organization. As a Business Transformation & Excellence Manager (m/f/x) you act as Daiichi Sankyo TechUnit Europe's neutral transformation integrator, accountable for moving selected priorities from diagnosis and design through implementation, adoption, stabilization and handover. The role reports directly to the Regional Head, TechUnit Europe, providing neutrality, authority and access across the organization. The TechUnit Europe Leadership Team prioritizes a small portfolio. Each initiative must have an executive sponsor, measurable outcomes, a defined scope and authority, and a named permanent owner. Critical interfaces: Regional Head, TechUnit Europe: sets mandate, provides authority and resolves priority conflicts Chief of Staff: ensures strategic coherence, portfolio governance, executive alignment and decision preparation; the transformation role converts selected decisions into adoption and sustained outcomes Executive sponsors: own business outcomes and major trade-offs TechUnit Europe Leadership Team: prioritizes the transformation portfolio and reviews progress Functions, Process Owners and sites: co-design and implement solutions, provide subject-matter expertise and retain permanent ownership Global stakeholders: align global direction with European operational realities and dependencies. Roles and Responsibilities Diagnose: Identify interface failures, accountability gaps, overlaps and root causes using data, stakeholder input and end-to-end process analysis Design: Co-create pragmatic future-state operating models, roles, governance, decision rights, handovers and ways of working.Implement: Build integrated plans and coordinate cross-functional, cross-site delivery across functions, Process Owners and local organizations Drive adoption: Lead stakeholder engagement, communication, capability building and behavior change so that new ways of working are used consistently Manage outcomes: Define measurable success criteria, track benefits, dependencies and risks, and provide transparent escalation to sponsors and the TechUnit Europe Leadership Team.Stabilize and hand over: Confirm that outcomes are embedded, close remaining gaps and transfer sustained ownership to a named function, Process Owner or site Strengthen transformation capability: Apply fit-for-purpose methods, reusable playbooks and lessons learned without creating unnecessary PMO bureaucracy.
