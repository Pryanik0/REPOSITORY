PV Lead Germany (m/f/d)

With a legacy of innovation since 1899, a presence in more than 30 countries, and more than 19,000 employees, we are advancing breakthrough therapies in oncology, cardiovascular disease, rare diseases, and immune disorders. Purpose of the Role: The PV Lead Germany is the main contact for pharmacovigilance activities in Germany and supports Austria and Switzerland. The role ensures compliance with local and global PV requirements, oversees local PV processes, and works closely with internal stakeholders, vendors, and global experts to support effective implementation of the global CSPV strategy. Roles and Responsibilities Act as the main PV contact for Germany and deputy nominated person for pharmacovigilance with German Health Authorities. Support and deputize the AT/CH Pharmacovigilance Cluster Lead for Austria and Switzerland, including acting as deputy national contact for Switzerland. Ensure compliant safety report collection and reporting to health authorities in the relevant countries. Create, maintain, and update local PV procedures in line with regulatory and global requirements. Serve as contact person for adverse event reporting from healthcare professionals, consumers, and pharmacists, including follow-up activities. Oversee local literature screening, implementation of Risk Management Plans (RMPs), and additional risk minimization measures. Monitor PV legislation updates and ensure local assessment and implementation of changes. Liaise with stakeholders and vendors, deliver PV training, and support compliant PV process implementation. Contribute to the EU PSMF, benefit-risk evaluations, authority responses, and audit/inspection activities. Oversee PV agreements, SDEAs, and local PV vendor management. Education and Experience: Bachelor’s degree required; Master’s degree in Life Sciences, Pharmacy, or Medical Sciences preferred. 2 to 5 years of PV experience with a Master’s degree required. 5 to 8 years of PV experience with a Bachelor’s degree required. Experience as a Local Safety Officer (LSO) preferred. Experience as Deputy Pharmacovigilance Cluster Lead or in a similar DACH PV role preferred. Leadership experience required. Strong knowledge of international, European, and local PV regulations required. Fluent German and proficient English required; French is a plus. Additional Requirements Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Proactive, reliable, and able to work independently. Good communication and stakeholder management skills.
