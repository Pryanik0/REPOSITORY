Summary of Job: The Senior Director, Global Medical/Scientific Communications leads the enterprise-wide strategy, operating model, and governance for global medical and scientific communications support for Medical Affairs, with Field Medical, congress communications, and external scientific engagement as core priorities. In partnership with Global Medical Strategy, Publications, Medical Education, Scientific Exchange, and regional and local Medical Affairs teams, the role translates approved strategies, scientific narratives, and evidence into clear, compelling, and compliant communications for global-to-local use. The Senior Director serves as global owner of Medical/Scientific Communications content within the defined scope of the role, directs external Medical Communications agencies and partners, and establishes standards that enable consistent, scalable, and effective communications across products, disease states, and channels. This Senior Director operates as an individual contributor and leads through influence across a complex global matrix. Essential Functions: Global Medical/Scientific Communications Strategy: Lead enterprise-wide medical/scientific communications strategy aligned with Global Medical Affairs priorities. Translate approved product and disease-state strategies, scientific platforms, and evidence into integrated global communications plans. Establish consistent scientific narratives, messaging frameworks, and content priorities across products, disease states, and channels. Anticipate emerging evidence, external trends, and Medical Affairs needs that may influence communications priorities. Scientific Content Development: Lead development of high-quality, compliant scientific content for Medical Affairs, with Field Medical, congress communications, and external scientific exchange as core priorities. Direct external Medical Communications agencies and partners through strategic briefs and scientific direction. Ensure content is accurate, balanced, appropriately referenced, modular, scalable, and suitable for efficient global-to-local adaptation and multichannel use. Establish scientific communication and visual standards for congress materials within scope, in partnership with Publications, Global Medical Strategy, and designated scientific owners. Strategic Interfaces: Publications: Partner with Publications and Global Medical Strategy owners to translate approved evidence and publication-derived messages into aligned downstream scientific communications, while Publications retains accountability for publication strategy and execution. Medical Education: Partner with Global and local Medical Teams to align global scientific communications strategy, approved scientific narratives, and relevant Medical Education activities, while Medical Teams/Medical Education retains accountability for its strategy and execution. External Scientific Engagement: Partner with Medical Strategy Teams, Strategic Capabilities & Scientific Exchange and Field Medical leadership to ensure global scientific communications strategy, narratives, and content enable high-quality, consistent, and compliant external scientific engagement. Global-to-Local Enablement: Engage global, regional and local Medical teams to incorporate relevant needs and insights into global communications planning. Provide approved source materials, adaptation guidance, and standards that enable efficient localization while preserving local accountability for approval and deployment. Medical Review and Content Governance: Establish governance for content development, review, approval, version control, distribution, maintenance, and retirement. Ensure scientific communications meet company policies and applicable legal, regulatory, privacy, and industry requirements. Provide enterprise standards and governance guidance for advisory board materials where global consistency improves efficiency; content development and execution remain locally owned. Partner with designated medical content owners, who retain accountability for scientific accuracy and interpretation. Agency and Operational Leadership: Provide strategic direction and performance oversight to external Medical Communications agencies and partners, including scope, quality, budgets, timelines, and delivery. Drive scalable processes and systems that improve content development, approval, localization, reuse, and lifecycle management. Retain accountability for communications strategy, content standards, governance, and delivery decisions within the role’s defined scope. Collaboration and Influence: Build strong partnerships across Global Medical Strategy Teams in each Therapeutic Area, regional and local Medical Affairs, Medical Information, Clinical Development, HEOR and Evidence Generation, Publications, Medical Education, Scientific Exchange, Legal, Regulatory, and Compliance. Lead cross-functional initiatives through influence, establish clear accountabilities, resolve competing priorities, and escalate scientific, operational, or compliance risks appropriately. Represent Global Medical/Scientific Communications in relevant governance and cross-functional forums. Measurement and Continuous Improvement: Establish outcome-focused measures for scientific quality, relevance, timeliness, adoption, reuse, stakeholder experience, and operational effectiveness. Use analytics, field insights, and stakeholder feedback to improve communications strategy and execution. Advance appropriate digital, automation, and AI-enabled capabilities while protecting scientific accuracy, compliance, confidentiality, and privacy. Subordinates : Senior individual contributor with enterprise-wide impact. Job Requirements: Education Advanced degree (PhD, PharmD, MD, or equivalent) in life sciences, medicine, pharmacy, public health, biomedical sciences, or a related scientific discipline. Additional education or specialized training in medical communications, scientific communications, medical affairs, healthcare communications, or business administration is highly preferred. Experience Minimum 12 years of progressive experience in Medical Affairs, Medical or Scientific Communications, Field Medical, Medical Information, or related pharmaceutical or biotechnology functions. Minimum five years leading global Medical/Scientific Communications strategy, Field Medical content strategy, or comparably complex scientific communications initiatives. Demonstrated ability to translate complex clinical evidence and approved scientific messages into compliant communications for Medical Affairs audiences and external scientific exchange. Strong background in global medical review processes and global-to-local content adaptation. Proven track record directing Medical Communications agencies and partners, including strategic briefing, quality oversight, budget management, and delivery. Broad experience supporting multiple therapeutic areas, products, geographies, or lifecycle stages. Field Medical expertise or substantial involvement in developing scientific content and resources to support Medical Science Liaison (MSL) activities. Technical Skills & Competencies Strong understanding of pharmaceutical industry requirements, regulatory standards, and compliance principles governing nonpromotional scientific exchange and Medical Affairs communications. Exceptional scientific writing, critical appraisal, and content development skills, with the ability to translate complex clinical and scientific evidence into clear, accurate, and compelling communications. Expertise in developing and deploying modular content strategies, omnichannel communications, and scalable global-to-local content approaches. Proficiency with content management platforms, digital communications channels, content analytics, and performance measurement tools. Knowledge of global medical content governance frameworks, medical review processes, approval workflows, and operational best practices. Understanding of emerging technologies, including responsible AI applications, to enhance content development, management, and communications effectiveness. Proven ability to operate independently, exercise sound judgment, manage ambiguity, and lead through influence in a global matrix. Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite and relevant content management, medical review, and collaboration platforms.
