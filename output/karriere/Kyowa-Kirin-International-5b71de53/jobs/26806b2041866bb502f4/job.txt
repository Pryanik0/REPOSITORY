Senior Oncology Medical Science Liaison- Southwest

Territory: Arizona, Utah, New Mexico, Texas, Colorado, Oklahoma, and Louisiana Location: Field-based. Candidates must live within the territory and near a major airport. Travel: Up to 70% About Kyowa Kirin Kyowa Kirin is a fast-growing global specialty pharmaceutical company that applies state-of-the-art biotechnology to discover and deliver novel medicines in four areas: bone and mineral disorders, intractable hematologic disorders, hematologic oncology, and rare disease. Based in Japan, we aim to make people smile by delivering breakthroughs where adequate treatments do not exist. Our work spans drug discovery, product development, and commercialization. Our North American headquarters is in Princeton, New Jersey, with offices in California, Massachusetts, and Ontario. Job Purpose The Senior Medical Science Liaison (MSL), Oncology is a field-based member of Kyowa Kirin’s Medical Affairs team and serves as a scientific and clinical expert for the assigned therapeutic area and products. The Senior MSL builds trusted relationships with healthcare professionals, key opinion leaders, researchers, and other external stakeholders to facilitate accurate, balanced, and evidence-based scientific exchange. The Senior MSL brings field insights to Medical Affairs to inform medical strategy, evidence generation, and cross-functional decision-making. The role operates in accordance with applicable regulations, industry standards, and company policies. Key Responsibilities Establish and maintain scientific relationships with national, regional, and local key opinion leaders, healthcare professionals, clinical investigators, and other external stakeholders. Engage in compliant, peer-to-peer scientific exchange on Kyowa Kirin products, disease states, and emerging clinical data. Respond to unsolicited requests for medical information. Identify and communicate actionable medical insights, unmet medical needs, and competitive intelligence to inform Medical Affairs strategy and evidence generation. Serve as the regional scientific expert and connect external stakeholders with appropriate Medical Affairs resources, initiatives, and opportunities for scientific collaboration. Support scientific engagement at medical congresses, advisory boards, investigator meetings, and other scientific forums. Build relationships with clinical investigators and partner with Clinical Operations to support investigator-initiated studies and company-sponsored clinical research. Develop and deliver scientific presentations, educational programs, and clinical resources for healthcare professionals and other appropriate stakeholders. Provide scientific training to internal partners on disease states, treatment landscapes, clinical data, and Kyowa Kirin products. Partner with Medical Affairs, Clinical Development, Commercial, Market Access, and Regulatory Affairs while maintaining appropriate boundaries between scientific and commercial activities. Communicate product safety information in accordance with company procedures, pharmacovigilance requirements, and applicable regulations. Stay current on clinical literature, treatment guidelines, and emerging data in the assigned therapeutic area. Contribute to Medical Affairs initiatives and strategic planning. Mentor fellow MSLs by sharing scientific expertise, field insights, and best practices. Job Requirements Education An advanced clinical or scientific degree or healthcare professional credential is required, such as an MD, DO, PhD, PharmD, NP, PA, MS, or equivalent. A doctoral degree (MD, DO, PhD, or PharmD) is preferred. Experience Three or more years of experience as an MSL, field-based medical professional, or in a related clinical, scientific, or Medical Affairs role is preferred. Experience in oncology or a related disease state is preferred. Demonstrated ability to engage healthcare professionals, key opinion leaders, and clinical investigators through compliant scientific exchange. Ability to interpret clinical and scientific data and communicate it effectively to different audiences. Familiarity with investigator-initiated studies or company-sponsored clinical trials is preferred. Experience with scientific congresses, advisory boards, investigator meetings, or other medical education activities is preferred. Demonstrated ability to manage field responsibilities, prioritize activities, and work independently in alignment with Medical Affairs objectives. Skills and Knowledge Knowledge of relevant disease states, treatment guidelines, and current standards of care. Familiarity with clinical research methods, evidence generation, and interpretation of clinical trial data. Working knowledge of FDA regulations, Good Clinical Practice, pharmacovigilance requirements, Office of Inspector General guidance, and applicable industry standards. Ability to evaluate medical literature, synthesize complex data, identify trends in field insights, and deliver accurate, balanced scientific presentations. Experience with Medical Affairs systems and customer relationship management platforms, along with proficiency in Microsoft Office. Strong written, verbal, listening, and presentation skills, with the ability to build working relationships and manage conflict effectively. Sound judgment, scientific integrity, and the ability to manage competing priorities and adapt in a fast-paced, multinational environment. Commitment to ongoing professional development and high ethical, legal, regulatory, and scientific standards. Travel and Work Requirements This field-based role requires up to 70% travel by air and ground, including overnight travel and occasional weekends for scientific congresses, advisory boards, and other Medical Affairs activities. A valid driver’s license and the ability to drive for business travel are required. The role may involve extended periods of sitting during travel and prolonged computer use.
