Senior Director, Medical Science Liaison Head

Kyowa Kirin is a fast-growing global specialty pharmaceutical company that applies state-of-the-art biotechnologies to discover and deliver novel medicines in four disease areas: bone and mineral; intractable hematologic; hematology oncology; and rare disease.  A Japan-based company, our goal is to make people smile by delivering breakthroughs where no adequate treatments currently exist, working from drug discovery to product development and commercialization. In North America, we are headquartered in Princeton, NJ, with offices in California, Massachusetts, and Ontario Summary of Job The Senior Director, Kyowa Kirin North America (KKNA) Medical Science Liaison Head provides strategic and operational leadership for Medical Science Liaison teams across KKNA therapeutic areas. This role establishes the enterprise MSL vision, operating model, and annual and long-range strategic plans; drives consistent, high-quality field execution; and develops field medical leadership capabilities. The Senior Director ensures that scientific exchange, external stakeholder engagement, and medical insights advance Medical Affairs and organizational priorities while meeting the highest standards of scientific integrity, ethics, and compliance. The role serves as a senior Medical Affairs leader and strategic partner, ensuring that field medical perspectives inform evidence generation, launch readiness, lifecycle planning, and organizational decision-making. Through a dotted-line relationship with the Global Head of Medical Strategic Capabilities and Scientific Exchange, the role ensures alignment of KKNA MSL strategy and execution with the Global Field Medical operating model and standards, while addressing regional needs and applicable local requirements. Essential Functions Leadership and Organizational Management Lead, coach, and develop MSL Directors, Associate Directors, and MSLs across therapeutic areas, establishing clear accountabilities, leadership expectations, succession plans, and talent-development priorities. Build and sustain a high-performing, inclusive, and collaborative field medical organization capable of operating effectively across therapeutic areas and within a complex matrix environment. Establish a performance-management framework that aligns team objectives, field execution standards, and outcomes with Medical Affairs priorities. Regularly evaluate organizational and individual performance, provide timely feedback, and implement development or improvement plans as appropriate. Define the MSL capability framework and proficiency expectations; identify organizational capability gaps; and oversee development plans that strengthen scientific expertise, insight generation, stakeholder engagement, and communication effectiveness. Ensure MSL teams remain current on product data, clinical evidence, scientific literature, industry developments, and regulations governing field medical activities. Lead organizational change and the adoption of new capabilities, technologies, processes, and ways of working that enhance field medical effectiveness. Strategic Planning and Execution Lead the development, prioritization, and execution of annual and long-range MSL strategies, operating plans, and budgets in alignment with therapeutic area, Medical Affairs, and enterprise objectives. Establish thought leader and healthcare professional engagement strategies that support meaningful scientific exchange and address defined medical and patient needs. Translate organizational priorities into clear field medical objectives, execution standards, resource plans, and measurable outcomes. Allocate people, budget, and technology resources based on strategic priorities, scientific opportunity, patient need, and anticipated field medical impact. Identify emerging opportunities and risks and recommend adjustments to strategy, operating plans, and resource deployment. Scientific Exchange, Stakeholder Engagement, and Medical Insights Provide leadership to MSL teams in building trusted scientific relationships with thought leaders, healthcare professionals, academic institutions, and other relevant external stakeholders. Ensure that external scientific exchange is evidence-based, balanced, responsive to stakeholder needs, and conducted in accordance with applicable policies and standards. Establish expectations and processes for the systematic collection, synthesis, escalation, communication, and application of field medical insights to ensure these inform evidence-generation plans, medical strategy, educational priorities, launch readiness, lifecycle planning, and organizational decision-making. Evaluate the quality and strategic relevance of stakeholder engagement and medical insights, not solely the volume of activity. Represent the MSL function in senior internal and external forums, as appropriate. Cross-Functional and Enterprise Leadership Serve as the senior MSL function partner to Medical Directors, Global Medical Affairs, Clinical Development, Research, Franchise, Commercial, Market Access, and other relevant leadership teams. Clarify roles, decision rights, and ways of working between MSL teams and cross-functional partners. Provide senior leadership with clear recommendations regarding field medical priorities, capabilities, organizational design, resource requirements, risks, and opportunities. Champion the strategic value of the MSL organization and communicate its contribution to Medical Affairs and enterprise objectives. Performance, Impact, and Continuous Improvement Own the MSL performance and impact framework, including the selection, implementation, monitoring, and communication of quantitative and qualitative measures that demonstrate execution quality, stakeholder value, insight impact, and progress against strategic objectives. Use data, analytics, and field intelligence to guide resource allocation, identify performance trends, and improve MSL effectiveness. Provide senior leadership with timely, balanced assessments of risks, resource needs, and opportunities. Sponsor continuous improvement initiatives that strengthen MSL processes, systems, governance, and stakeholder experience. Governance, Ethics, and Compliance Set and model high standards and ensure that MSL activities adhere to applicable laws, regulations, policies, standard operating procedures, and Medical Affairs governance requirements. Ensure teams receive appropriate training and understand their individual accountability for compliant execution. Identify, escalate, and address compliance, quality, operational, or reputational risks associated with field medical activities and partner with Legal, Regulatory, Compliance, and other control functions to strengthen governance and resolve identified gaps. Education A doctoral level scientific or clinical degree, such as MD, PharmD, or PhD, is preferred. A relevant advanced degree or professional qualification in the life sciences, nursing, pharmacy, medicine, or a related discipline is required. Experience A minimum of 10 years of progressive experience in Medical Affairs or field medical leadership. At least five years of experience leading managers and geographically dispersed medical teams. Demonstrated success developing and executing field medical strategy across multiple therapeutic areas, products, or lifecycle stages. Experience managing budgets, organizational resources, operating plans, and competing strategic priorities. Proven ability to partner with senior cross-functional and global leaders in a complex matrix organization. Demonstrated experience leading organizational change, capability development, and continuous-improvement initiatives. Experience supporting launch readiness, evidence generation, medical insights, and lifecycle planning is strongly preferred. Proven success leading leaders, building high-performing teams, developing talent, and strengthening succession pipelines.
