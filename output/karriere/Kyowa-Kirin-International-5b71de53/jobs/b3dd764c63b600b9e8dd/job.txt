Summary: The Senior Specialist, Digital Quality (Quality Systems & Compliance) serves as the Quality business lead and system owner for laboratory digital systems, including LabVantage LIMS, analytical instrument platforms, and related interfaces supporting GxP operations at Kyowa Kirin’s Sanford, NC manufacturing site. This role ensures laboratory computerized systems remain compliant, validated, inspection-ready, and aligned with regulatory requirements, including 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, ALCOA++, and data integrity standards. Partnering with IT, QC Operations, Automation, Engineering, system vendors, and global stakeholders, the incumbent oversees system lifecycle management, validation, change control, user access, periodic reviews, enhancements, and continuous improvement. As the business administrator for LabVantage and related systems, this role translates business requirements into compliant digital solutions, supports system integrations and investigations, and ensures the reliable use of laboratory data and technology. This position reports to the Sr. Manager, Quality Systems & Compliance and receives day-to-day Digital Quality priorities, technical direction, and workstream alignment from the Digital Quality Lead, as applicable. Essential Functions: Technical Skills, Knowledge, and Experience •    Serve as Quality System Owner and Business Administrator for LabVantage LIMS and associated laboratory computerized systems, ensuring systems remain compliant, validated, fit for intended use, and aligned with business requirements. •    Partner with Information Technology, laboratory operations, Automation, Engineering, global teams, and system vendors to manage the lifecycle of laboratory digital systems, including implementation, configuration, validation, maintenance, upgrades, integrations, and retirement. •    Act as the primary Quality business representative for laboratory digital platforms by translating laboratory and compliance requirements into user requirements, system configurations, enhancements, and sustainable operational solutions. •    Maintain system ownership and lifecycle documentation, including user requirements, system inventories, data flow diagrams, validation plans, risk assessments, traceability documentation, periodic reviews, and system governance records. •    Author, review, and approve computerized system validation deliverables, including protocols, test scripts, reports, risk assessments, deviations, and technical documentation, using a risk-based approach and ensure laboratory computerized systems comply with data integrity and electronic records and electronic signatures requirements, including 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, ALCOA++, and applicable global, regional, and site procedures. •    Lead and coordinate system periodic reviews, user access reviews, audit trail review governance, backup and recovery assessments, and other controls required to maintain the validated state and inspection readiness. •    Partner with IT and vendors to manage incidents, deviations, corrective and preventive actions, change controls, enhancements, upgrades, cybersecurity activities, and service delivery affecting GxP laboratory systems. •    Provide Quality oversight and business administration for analytical instrument software and associated interfaces to ensure secure, reliable, and compliant generation, processing, review, retention, and retrieval of laboratory data. •    Support the implementation, validation, and integration of analytical instruments with LIMS, chromatography data systems, and other enterprise platforms while maintaining data integrity and intended-use requirements. •    Collaborate with laboratory subject matter experts and process owners to optimize workflows, standardize master data and system use, improve data management practices, and implement technology-enabled process improvements. •    Monitor and communicate system health, compliance risks, project status, service performance, and quality metrics to Quality leadership and relevant stakeholder and serve as a subject matter expert during regulatory inspections, client audits, and internal audits for LIMS, analytical instrument systems, computerized system lifecycle controls, and laboratory data integrity. •    Author, review, and maintain procedures, work instructions, governance documents, system administration guides, and training materials related to digital quality systems and deliver training, coaching, and ongoing business support to system users and administrators to promote consistent, compliant, and effective use of laboratory digital systems. Non-Technical Skills: Demonstrates a results-oriented approach by setting clear objectives, managing priorities, and delivering measurable outcomes aligned with organizational goals. Navigates ambiguity and change effectively while maintaining accountability, responsiveness, and attention to detail. Master’s degree in related scientific or technical discipline (preferred). Experience •    Minimum of 5 years of experience in a GxP-regulated pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or related life sciences environment, with a focus on Quality Assurance for computerized systems, laboratory systems, or digital platforms. •    Demonstrated experience serving as a system owner, business administrator, validation lead, or Quality representative for GxP computerized systems. •    Experience with Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS); hands-on experience with LabVantage is strongly preferred. •    Experience supporting analytical instrument qualification, analytical instrument software validation, system integration, and lifecycle management. •    Demonstrated experience in computerized system validation, including authoring or reviewing user requirements, protocols, test scripts, risk assessments, traceability documentation, and validation reports. •    Experience partnering with Information Technology teams, vendors, and business stakeholders to implement, administer, maintain, and improve GxP systems, along with a strong understanding of data integrity requirements, electronic records and electronic signatures, 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, ALCOA++, and global regulatory expectations for computerized systems. •    Experience managing deviations, investigations, CAPAs, change controls, periodic reviews, user access reviews, and audit readiness activities associated with GxP systems, and experience supporting regulatory inspections or audits involving laboratory computerized systems, validation, or data integrity. •    Experience with laboratory informatics ecosystems, including LIMS, chromatography data systems such as Empower, analytical instrument software, interfaces, and master data governance, preferred, and experience supporting facility start-up, system implementation, technical transfer, or operational readiness activities is highly desirable. •    Demonstrated written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to author SOPs, training materials, governance documents, and technical reports.
