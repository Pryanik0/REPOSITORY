Our culture is rooted in our values: Teamwork, Commitment to Life, Innovation, and Integrity. They help us to push boundaries to deliver extraordinary impact and make KKI a brilliant place to work. Job Purpose Provide executive leadership for an integrated Pricing, Value, Access and Government/Public Affairs agenda across the Northern Cluster, ensuring they are embedded in in-line portfolio, launch assets, lifecycle and business planning. The role is accountable for securing and sustaining optimal access to Rare Disease medicines, protecting long-term value and shaping a supportive policy and funding environment. The role advises the Cluster General Manager and leadership team, influences regional and global strategy, and makes or recommends high-impact decisions across investment, evidence generation, pricing, sequencing, negotiation, policy positioning, government relations, corporate communications and external risk. The role provides line leadership for the Cluster Patient Advocacy role, ensuring patient insight and the patient voice inform access and policy priorities, while safeguarding the independence,
