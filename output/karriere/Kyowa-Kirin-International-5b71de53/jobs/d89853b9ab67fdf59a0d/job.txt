The ideal candidate will possess strong knowledge of GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices), facility and equipment maintenance, and regulatory compliance. This role is critical to ensuring the reliable operation of essential systems, minimizing downtime, and supporting a clean, compliant production environment. As an individual contributor, you will take a compassionate and people-focused approach to your work. You will proactively collaborate with others to create a supportive and inclusive environment, always acting in line with the organization’s values.
