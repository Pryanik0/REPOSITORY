Job Purpose You will support the MAGO (Medical Affairs Governance & Operations) team by coordinating the medical governance secretariat, contributing to the management, implementation and monitoring of written standards, and supporting regional medical risk management activities under appropriate senior oversight Responsibilities Key Responsibilities: Coordinate the monthly Medical Governance Committee cycle, including collating topics, preparing agendas and pre-reads, facilitating meeting logistics, and drafting minutes and action logs for review Maintain the MGC communication plan and platform, proposing improvements for senior approval Contribute analysis and recommendations to support MAGO prioritisation of regional medical written standards Conduct gap analyses of existing written standards and draft recommendations for the evolving regional medical written standards landscape Support the ongoing development and operation of the internal control framework for regional medical written standards Support the Regional Risk Team and coordinate assigned risk management activities Act as an operational Medical Affairs contact for the regional risk team Qualifications Position Requirements Life sciences degree to Batchelors level Strong working knowledge of Medical Affairs and pharmaceutical medical governance, Demonstrated experience of the external regulatory landscape relevant to Medical Affairs activities Practical understanding of risk management, internal controls and governance processes, with relevant hands-on experience This role will be based in Marlow but with international travel for meetings with affiliate teams. Driving and overnight stays will be required Kyowa Kirin International is an equal opportunities employer. No agencies please.
