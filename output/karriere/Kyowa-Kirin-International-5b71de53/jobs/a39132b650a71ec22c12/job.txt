Job Purpose Provide regulatory leadership across promotional / non promotional activities and the product lifecycle. Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and internal standards. Serve as Deputy Responsible Pharmacist when required. Responsibilities Key Responsibilities: · Lead regulatory activities across the product lifecycle, including early access, labelling, packaging . Ensure regulatory compliance of promotional and non-promotional materials, events, digital channels and media communications. Oversee promotional information activities , ensuring compliance with promotional information charter. Partner with Medical, Marketing and Commercial teams to embed regulatory best practice and drive continuous improvement. Manage relationships with health and regulatory authorities on product portfolio and promotional matters. Lead regulatory training and governance , ensuring teams have the knowledge, tools and guidance needed to operate compliantly. Monitor regulatory developments , assess business impact and coordinate appropriate action plans. Act as Deputy Responsible Pharmacist when required. Qualifications Position Requirements Pharmacist registered with the CNOP, with Deputy Responsible Pharmacist eligibility Experience in promotional material approval and early access coordination Strong knowledge of promotional information requirements 10+ years’ pharmaceutical industry experience, ideally within an exploitant/manufacturing environment. Fluent operational English. Analytical, adaptable and solution-focused, with strong teamwork and decision-making skills. Compensation: Salary range based on your experience between 75,000.0 Eur – 100,000.0 Eur / annum
