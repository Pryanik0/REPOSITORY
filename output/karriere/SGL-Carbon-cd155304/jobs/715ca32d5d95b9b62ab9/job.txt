Maintenance Assistant

Search Criteria Loading... Apply now » Apply now Apply Now Start Please wait... Maintenance Assistant Role Purpose To maintain fully functional Cutting heads to ensure Production targets are met. Key Accountabilities Strip down, clean and reassemble Finn & Fram Chopping heads to ensure full functionality. Identify and refer any changes in Chopping head conditions to minimise wear and damage. Maintain records of Chopping head condition and consumable usage to comply with preventive maintenance programme. Change anvil sleeves and turn down utilising lathe. Perform other duties within Engineering within own level of competence to maintain operational effectiveness. Undertake site-wide unskilled activities to maintain required housekeeping and Health & Safety standards. Support Stores during peak times of cycle counts & stocktaking. Special Aspects of the Position On occasions will be required to assist skilled Maintenance Technicians in the performance of more complex activities. Corporate Definition Responsible for the cleanliness of blade change and lathe work area. Consumable parts management.  Blades etc. Reliability of blade change equipment. Support of Ops manager in defining the maintenance efforts and strategy according to company needs. Preferred Knowledge / Skills / Experiences Fluency in written & spoken English. A strong work ethic. Standard Grade English and Maths or equivalent. Proven experience of semi-skilled mechanical engineering activities in a production environment. Experience in working to statutory and company requirements on Health & Safety, Quality and Environment. Adaptability. Evidence of setting high work standards and a commitment to quality.
