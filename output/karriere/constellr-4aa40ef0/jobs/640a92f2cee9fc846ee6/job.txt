Senior Bids & Proposals Manager

Back to jobs New Senior Bids & Proposals Manager Berlin | Hybrid; Munich | Hybrid Apply Job description Team & Reporting Line: Program Management, reporting to the VP Program Management Our Mission At constellr, we use thermal intelligence from space to understand what is happening on Earth - where, when and at scale. Our satellite constellation delivers high-resolution land surface temperature data, giving governments, defence organisations, commercial operators and global institutions the intelligence they need to monitor change, assess risk and make better-informed decisions. From national security and critical infrastructure to agriculture and climate resilience, constellr turns temperature into actionable intelligence for a changing world. Your Role We are seeking a technically strong Bids & Proposals Manager to place constellr's space systems in government and institutional programs, and to lead those bids through to submission. The focus is upstream and midstream: our current and next-generation payloads, satellites, and system capabilities, including ground segment and data processing. Downstream applications are handled by a dedicated partnering team, with whom you will coordinate where a bid requires it. This role combines deep technical understanding with disciplined bid execution. You will know our payload and system roadmap well enough to identify where it fits in national, European, defense, and multilateral programs. You will shape our positioning before tenders are published, and then own the bid once we commit, writing the technical core of the proposal yourself. Key responsibilities Build a thorough understanding of constellr's current and next-generation payload, satellite, and system capabilities, and track how they evolve as engineering develops them. Identify government and institutional programs (space agencies, defense ministries, national and European missions, multilateral programs) where constellr's systems can be placed, and map our capabilities against their requirements. Position constellr with agencies, ministries, and program offices before tenders are published, through technical briefings, industry days, and early requirement discussions, in collaboration with our Government Solutions team. Run bid/no-bid assessments with leadership, grounded in a realistic view of technical fit, delivery risk, and win probability. Own the bid process end to end once a pursuit is committed: strategy, win themes, compliance matrix, schedule, and submission. Write and edit the technical core of proposals yourself (system and payload descriptions, technical compliance, performance and architecture narratives). Work closely with engineering, program management, and finance on management approach, schedule, and pricing. Ensure every submission is fully compliant with the solicitation's requirements, whatever the format or jurisdiction. Lead teaming and subcontractor coordination where bids require industrial partners, and interface with the downstream partnering team where applications are part of the offer. Build reusable content over time (templates, past performance write-ups, win themes) so each new bid starts from a stronger base. Support a smooth handover into program execution once a contract is won, so that what was proposed can be delivered. Job requirements About you Required: 7+ years of experience in bid management, capture management, or business development for government or institutional contracts, with a track record of wins you can point to. A strong technical background in space systems, ideally with an engineering or physics degree and hands-on exposure to payloads, satellite platforms, or ground segment. A solid understanding of how institutional and defense space programs are structured and procured (e.g., ESA, national agencies, defense procurement, multilateral programs). Strong technical writing: you can turn complex payload and system capabilities into a clear, compliant, persuasive proposal yourself, not just manage the people who do. Disciplined execution: you run bids to schedule, keep compliance tight, and draw input from engineering and finance. A natural representative presence: ministry- and agency-level stakeholders take you seriously and want you in the room. Comfort working across teams in a small, fast-moving company without a dedicated proposal department behind you. Willingness to travel internationally and work across time zones. Experience with thermal infrared or optical Earth observation missions is highly desirable. Existing relationships with European, Gulf, or Asia-Pacific space agencies, defense ministries, or civil government bodies are a strong plus. Familiarity with formal bid methodologies (e.g., Shipley) and ECSS or equivalent space standards is a plus. A second or third language relevant to our target markets is a plus. Why join constellr? High-Impact National Security Mission: Be part of an ambitious mission to launch the world’s first commercial thermal infrared constellation, delivering sovereign-grade "behavioural" intelligence from space to close critical blind spots for defence, intelligence, and maritime surveillance agencies. Fast-Paced "New Space" Innovation: Work at the leading edge of a rapidly evolving defence sector. By leveraging modern, agile New Space methodologies, you will help design, build, and deploy dual-use thermal payloads into operational orbit at a speed and scale that traditional aerospace models simply cannot match. Broad Interdisciplinary Exposure: Accelerate your professional growth by collaborating daily with a diverse, international cohort of astrophysicists, software developers, and defence-system engineers. Inclusive & Scientific Culture: Join a high-trust team deeply committed to diversity, inclusion, and scientific excellence, where academic curiosity is paired with high autonomy and a flat hierarchy. Financial Upside & Well-being: Benefit from a Virtual Share Option Plan (VSOP) for permanent employees, giving you a true stake in the company's growth, alongside highly flexible hybrid working models.
