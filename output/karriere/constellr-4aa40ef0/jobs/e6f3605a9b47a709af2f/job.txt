Head of EU Institutional Relations (Managing Director, Belgium)

Back to jobs Head of EU Institutional Relations (Managing Director, Belgium) Belgium | Remote Apply Job description Team & Reporting Line: Chief Strategy Officer (CSO) | Sovereign & Strategy Team Our Mission At constellr, we're harnessing the power of space to solve one of Earth’s greatest challenges: climate resilience & security in a changing world. Our thermal satellite constellation delivers the most accurate land surface temperature data on the market, equipping commercial players, governments, defence agencies, and global institutions with the thermal intelligence they need to make strategic decisions – from national security and infrastructure to agriculture and climate resilience. This is not a concept on a slide. Our first two SkyBee satellites have been in orbit since 2025, each flying a cryocooled thermal infrared instrument on a microsatellite, and our next satellites are already in the pipeline. Backed by a €37M Series A, we are now opening a new site in Munich to bring assembly, integration and test (AIT) in-house. With offices in Brussels, Freiburg, Munich, Berlin and Toulouse, our multidisciplinary team of engineers, scientists and strategists is turning flight data from orbit into the next generation of thermal satellites. Your Role You are constellr’s proactive voice and representative in Brussels, and our dealmaker with the European institutions. Your brief is to ensure thermal is recognised as the third Earth observation domain alongside optical and radar in the next EU budget, and to turn that recognition into funded data contracts and programmes. You know your way around the Commission, the European Parliament, Member State representations and ESA, you know which budget line pays for what, and you measure yourself in qualified, funded opportunities and relevant budget lines, not meetings held. Sitting in our Sovereign & Strategy team, you will work closely with Sales & Bid Management and Grant Management to coordinate submissions to EC-funded programmes, and with our regional country managers, Government Affairs, Strategic Communications and Product & Engineering. The role will also include the responsibilities of Managing Director of constellr’s Belgian entity. Key responsibilities Own constellr’s relationships with the European Commission (DG DEFIS, Copernicus, EUSPA etc.) and support our outreach via national lines, incl. DLR, CNES and BELSPO, to ESA and others. Lobby for thermal in the 2028–2034 Multiannual Financial Framework, EU EOGS, the Copernicus Contributing Missions and ESA’s ERS programme: your goal is to embed explicit requirements, a dedicated purchasing line and recurring data buys. Build a coalition of Member State delegations, MEPs, trade associations and industry partners who will collaborate on our collective narrative. Run the European institutional capture plan: qualify each opportunity on need, budget, route and timing, align it with our national capture plans, and oversee the handover to the bid team once a concrete opportunity is identified. Spot calls, tenders and policy shifts early, and brief leadership on the implications for the business and the right actions to prepare. Steer institutional bids and grant applications (Horizon Europe, CASSINI, EDF, ESA) so they are well aligned and targeted, working closely with our Strategic Partnerships Lead and ESA Liaison to form effective consortia. Cement constellr’s position by publishing policy papers, preparing consultation responses, organising targeted events and drafting actionable briefings for leadership. Ensure constellr’s executives meet the right people, at the right level, at the right moment. Open the Benelux markets where they add value – Belgian, Dutch and Luxembourg defence, space and security buyers – and coordinate Belgian access to ESA and EU programmes. Managing Director responsibilities: lead the Belgian entity as Managing Director, with responsibility for all legal, tax and HR topics and ownership of the Belgian P&L, ensuring all advocacy remains compliant with the EU Transparency Register and export control rules. What success looks like after 12 months Thermal IR named alongside optical and radar in MFF or programme drafting, with written institutional support on file. A pipeline of qualified, budgeted EU (and ESA) opportunities accepted by Sales, reported by number and addressable value. constellr inside the consortia and studies that shape the 2027–28 procurements. At least one qualified Benelux opportunity or partnership opened through the Belgian entity. Job requirements About you You have sat on both sides of the table: you have sold to institutions and you have worked in advocacy. You are commercially sharp, politically fluent and impatient with activity that does not move a budget – and highly motivated to drive change and win in a complex, often intangible public market. Required: 10+ years across government business development and EU public affairs, ideally in space, defence, aerospace or deep tech. A live Brussels network: Commission, ESA, Parliament, Permanent Representations. Ability to open doors in Belgium, the Netherlands and Luxembourg: ministries, space offices and primes. A track record of turning institutional access and engagement into contracts, funding or programme language. Working command of how EU and ESA money flows (MFF, SAFE, Copernicus, Horizon Europe, ESA subscriptions and geo-return) and experience building strategies to access it. A clear, persuasive writer and speaker, credible with Directors-General, budget holders and engineers alike. Fluent English. Nice to Have : Business-level German, French and/or Italian. Earth observation or satellite data background. Experience with EDF, SAFE or other defence instruments. Time spent inside an EU institution, ESA or a national delegation. Advocacy or business development experience within a defence or deep-tech business. Why join constellr? High-Impact National Security Mission: Be part of an ambitious mission to launch the world’s first commercial thermal infrared constellation, delivering sovereign-grade "behavioural" intelligence from space to close critical blind spots for defence, intelligence, and maritime surveillance agencies. Fast-Paced "New Space" Innovation: Work at the leading edge of a rapidly evolving defence sector. By leveraging modern, agile New Space methodologies, you will help design, build, and deploy dual-use thermal payloads into operational orbit at a speed and scale that traditional aerospace models simply cannot match. Broad Interdisciplinary Exposure: Accelerate your professional growth by collaborating daily with a diverse, international cohort of astrophysicists, software developers, and defence-system engineers. Inclusive & Scientific Culture: Join a high-trust team deeply committed to diversity, inclusion, and scientific excellence, where academic curiosity is paired with high autonomy and a flat hierarchy. Financial Upside & Well-being: Benefit from a Virtual Share Option Plan (VSOP) for permanent employees, giving you a true stake in the company's growth, alongside highly flexible hybrid working models.
