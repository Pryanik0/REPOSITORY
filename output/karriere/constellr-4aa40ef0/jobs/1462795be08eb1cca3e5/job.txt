Financial Planning Data Analyst

Back to jobs Financial Planning Data Analyst Berlin | Hybrid; Munich | Hybrid Apply Job description Team & Reporting Line: Finance Team, Reporting to VP Operations Our Mission At constellr, we use thermal intelligence from space to understand what is happening on Earth - where, when and at scale. Our satellite constellation delivers high-resolution land surface temperature data, giving governments, defence organisations, commercial operators and global institutions the intelligence they need to monitor change, assess risk and make better-informed decisions. From national security and critical infrastructure to agriculture and climate resilience, constellr turns temperature into actionable intelligence for a changing world. Your Role You'll build the financial models and dashboards that power constellr's planning and decision-making, turning raw data into clear, actionable financial insight . Key responsibilities Build and maintain FP&A financial models Develop reporting dashboards and automate recurring reports Support budgeting and forecasting cycles with data analysis Partner with Business Controlling on KPI tracking Identify opportunities for reporting automation and efficiency Job requirements About you Required: Experience in FP&A, financial analysis or data analytics Strong Excel/financial modeling skills; familiarity with BI tools Ability to translate data into clear recommendations Fluent German and English Nice to Have: Experience with Power BI, Tableau or similar SQL or scripting skills for data automation Experience in a scale-up finance function Why join constellr? High-Impact National Security Mission: Be part of an ambitious mission to launch the world’s first commercial thermal infrared constellation, delivering sovereign-grade "behavioural" intelligence from space to close critical blind spots for defence, intelligence, and maritime surveillance agencies. Fast-Paced "New Space" Innovation: Work at the leading edge of a rapidly evolving defence sector. By leveraging modern, agile New Space methodologies, you will help design, build, and deploy dual-use thermal payloads into operational orbit at a speed and scale that traditional aerospace models simply cannot match. Broad Interdisciplinary Exposure: Accelerate your professional growth by collaborating daily with a diverse, international cohort of astrophysicists, software developers, and defence-system engineers. Inclusive & Scientific Culture: Join a high-trust team deeply committed to diversity, inclusion, and scientific excellence, where academic curiosity is paired with high autonomy and a flat hierarchy. Financial Upside & Well-being: Benefit from a Virtual Share Option Plan (VSOP) for permanent employees, giving you a true stake in the company's growth, alongside highly flexible hybrid working models.
