Back to jobs Students & Interns Munich | On-site Apply Are you a student looking for an internship, a working student (Werkstudent) role or your thesis project in the space industry? We’d love to hear from you. About constellr At constellr, we build and operate our own constellation of thermal infrared satellites. From orbit, we measure the temperature of the Earth’s surface with high precision and turn that data into insights that help our customers make better decisions, from agriculture and water management to climate resilience, infrastructure and security. We’re a fast-growing European New Space company with teams in Germany and Belgium and our main hub in Munich. Our people work across everything it takes to run a satellite business: designing missions, flying satellites, processing imagery, building software and bringing our data to customers around the world. Why join us as a student? You’ll do real work that matters, not coffee runs. You’ll work alongside experienced engineers, scientists and business experts who are happy to share what they know. You’ll see how a space company works from the inside, from satellite operations to product and commercial teams. And you’ll get the chance to grow with us, since many great hires start as students. Who we’re looking for You’re currently enrolled at a university or college, or about to start an internship, working student position or thesis. You’re curious, motivated and keen to learn. Your field doesn’t have to be aerospace: we have opportunities across engineering, data, software, operations, finance, people, communications and more. How to apply Join our Students & Interns talent community by completing the short application form below. To help us get your profile in front of the right team: Upload your CV. Make sure it includes your field of study and expected graduation date. Choose the team you’re most interested in from the dropdown list. Tell us where you’d be able to work. What happens next? Your profile will be shared with the team you’ve chosen. This isn’t an application for a specific role. Instead, whenever an internship, working student or thesis opportunity opens that matches your interests, we’ll reach out to you directly. We review every profile, but because we receive many applications, we may not be able to respond to everyone individually. Your privacy We’ll keep your details for up to 24 months so we can contact you about future opportunities. You can withdraw your consent and have your data deleted at any time by emailing dpo@constellr.com . Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.
