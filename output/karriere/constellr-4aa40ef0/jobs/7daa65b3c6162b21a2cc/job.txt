Senior Infrastructure Operations Engineer

Back to jobs Senior Infrastructure Operations Engineer Munich | On-site Apply Job description Team & Reporting Line: IT Team, reporting to the IT Director Our Mission At constellr, we use thermal intelligence from space to understand what is happening on Earth - where, when and at scale. Our satellite constellation delivers high-resolution land surface temperature data, giving governments, defence organisations, commercial operators and global institutions the intelligence they need to monitor change, assess risk and make better-informed decisions. From national security and critical infrastructure to agriculture and climate resilience, constellr turns temperature into actionable intelligence for a changing world. Your Role You'll design and maintain the infrastructure backbone that keeps constellr's operations reliable and scalable, from our office networks to the server room that supports our satellite data. This is a hands-on role with a high degree of ownership, right at the core of our technical operations. Key responsibilities Design, deploy and maintain our network and server infrastructure Take ownership of capacity planning across our compute, storage and network resources Lead our response to infrastructure incidents, identifying root causes and resolving them Maintain clear documentation and standard operating procedures for our infrastructure Evaluate and implement new infrastructure technologies as the company scales Job requirements About you Required: Strong hands-on experience in network and systems infrastructure engineering Experience with server room or data center operations Solid troubleshooting skills and experience responding to incidents Fluency in English Nice to Have : German language skills Experience in a scientific, aerospace or data-intensive environment Experience with cloud infrastructure such as AWS, Azure or GCP Relevant certifications, such as CCNA or CompTIA Why join constellr? High-Impact National Security Mission: Be part of an ambitious mission to launch the world’s first commercial thermal infrared constellation, delivering sovereign-grade "behavioural" intelligence from space to close critical blind spots for defence, intelligence, and maritime surveillance agencies. Fast-Paced "New Space" Innovation: Work at the leading edge of a rapidly evolving defence sector. By leveraging modern, agile New Space methodologies, you will help design, build, and deploy dual-use thermal payloads into operational orbit at a speed and scale that traditional aerospace models simply cannot match. Broad Interdisciplinary Exposure: Accelerate your professional growth by collaborating daily with a diverse, international cohort of astrophysicists, software developers, and defence-system engineers. Inclusive & Scientific Culture: Join a high-trust team deeply committed to diversity, inclusion, and scientific excellence, where academic curiosity is paired with high autonomy and a flat hierarchy. Financial Upside & Well-being: Benefit from a Virtual Share Option Plan (VSOP) for permanent employees, giving you a true stake in the company's growth, alongside highly flexible hybrid working models.
