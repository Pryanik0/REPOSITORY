Mission Control Engineer

Back to jobs Mission Control Engineer Germany | Remote Apply Job description Team & Reporting Line: Flight Operations - Reporting to Lead Satellite Systems Our Mission At constellr, we use thermal intelligence from space to understand what is happening on Earth - where, when and at scale. Our satellite constellation delivers high-resolution land surface temperature data, giving governments, defence organisations, commercial operators and global institutions the intelligence they need to monitor change, assess risk and make better-informed decisions. From national security and critical infrastructure to agriculture and climate resilience, constellr turns temperature into actionable intelligence for a changing world. Your Role As a Mission Control Engineer , you will be central to operating and scaling our Earth Observation satellite constellation. Bringing deep domain expertise in mission control, you will monitor, control, and optimise spacecraft performance while working closely with the Flight Operations team and collaborating with Flight Dynamics, Payload, Software, Data, and Product teams across the company. In this senior, hands-on role, you will act as a team lead, providing both technical and people leadership to a group of mission control engineers while continuing to contribute directly to the design, build, and operation of our systems. You will set technical direction, mentor and develop the team, and help build mission control systems from the ground up in a fast-paced startup environment, with direct impact on the reliability, availability, and quality of the data we deliver to customers. At constellr, you will help shape how our constellation and operations scale, owning critical operational capabilities, influencing system design, and seeing the immediate real-world impact of your work. The role also offers the opportunity to take on real-time spacecraft commanding and monitoring responsibilities and contribute directly to mission success in orbit.
