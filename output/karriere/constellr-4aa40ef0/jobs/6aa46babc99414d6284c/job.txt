Fast-Forward Engineer - Geospatial AI & Intelligent Products

Back to jobs New Fast-Forward Engineer - Geospatial AI & Intelligent Products Berlin | Hybrid; Munich | Hybrid Apply Job description Team & Reporting Line: Intelligence | Data & Analytics Our Mission At constellr, we use thermal intelligence from space to understand what is happening on Earth - where, when and at scale. Our satellite constellation delivers high-resolution land surface temperature data, giving governments, defence organisations, commercial operators and global institutions the intelligence they need to monitor change, assess risk and make better-informed decisions. From national security and critical infrastructure to agriculture and climate resilience, constellr turns temperature into actionable intelligence for a changing world. Your Role We are building the next generation of thermal satellites. This is not a concept on a slide: our first two SkyBee satellites have been in orbit since 2025, each flying a cryocooled thermal infrared instrument on a microsatellite, and, backed by a €37M Series A, our next satellites are already in the pipeline. But the value of that data comes from what we can build with it. That’s where you come in. In Data & Analytics, we are building the team that will turn this data into AI-powered intelligence products. You will help shape our first solutions across all customer segments: for defence and security customers in National Security and Maritime Awareness; for public-sector customers in Urban Dynamics and Food & Water Security; and for commercial customers in Industrial Monitoring, delivered directly and through partner platforms. Beyond that, you will help lay the groundwork for multi-domain intelligence integration, for example as part of a European sovereign space intelligence consortium. Join constellr as a Fast-Forward Engineer for Geospatial AI & Intelligent Products and help turn ideas from Product, Intelligence, customers and domain teams into working systems. You will be a hands-on builder, rapidly creating MVPs that combine constellr’s thermal Earth observation data, external information sources and modern AI capabilities. Working closely with Product, Intelligence, Data Platforms, customer-facing teams and early users, you will translate requirements and ideas into prototypes, APIs and workbenches that can be tested and validated. One week you might build a facility research workbench combining satellite observations, OpenStreetMap, external datasets and web-derived context; another, you might expose a new thermal indicator through an API, AI agent or lightweight analytical application. Success is measured by how quickly we learn what works, what creates customer value and what deserves to move toward production, not by how much prototype code survives. Key responsibilities Rapidly turn requirements, ideas and problems from Product, Insights, customers and other domain teams into working MVPs, APIs and workbenches, helping validate technical feasibility, test ideas with users and accelerate the path toward production. Build AI-powered workflows using LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), tool/function calling, structured outputs and agents where they add value, choosing the simplest architecture that can answer the product question. Build end-to-end prototypes across data ingestion, data processing, backend APIs and lightweight user interfaces, using AI-assisted engineering to shorten the path from idea to working service. Create data-fusion and geospatial intelligence workflows that combine constellr observations with external geospatial, commercial, open-source and web-derived information. Develop geospatial search, facility research, monitoring and analytical capabilities using Python, PostgreSQL/PostGIS and modern geospatial libraries and services. Deploy and operate experiments independently in sandbox/cloud environments using Docker and lightweight infrastructure, with working knowledge of Linux and container orchestration sufficient to make prototypes available in days or weeks rather than months. Work closely with domain experts and early users, gather feedback, and evaluate prototypes against technical feasibility, user value, data availability, accuracy, operating cost and scalability; translate the evidence into clear recommendations for Product and Technology. Define a clean graduation path for successful experiments: document assumptions and architecture, identify reusable components and collaborate with production engineering teams when a concept is ready to become a supported product. Actively discard or simplify experiments that do not demonstrate value, keeping the innovation environment fast, pragmatic and free from unnecessary production complexity. Job requirements About you Required: Strong hands-on software engineering skills in Python (4+ years), with experience building and deploying real software systems and the ability to independently take a service from data ingestion to API or user interface. Hands-on experience applying modern AI/LLM capabilities to software products, including RAG, tool/function calling, structured outputs, prompt design or agentic workflows. Experience with data-intensive systems, plus confidence building data pipelines and integrating multiple APIs and heterogeneous data sources. Ability to build lightweight services, for example with FastAPI, and enough frontend capability to create usable analytical tools, workbenches or dashboards. A builder and product mindset: comfortable with ambiguity, able to work directly with users and domain experts, make pragmatic technical choices, iterate from feedback and distinguish prototype quality from production quality. Experience working effectively with agentic coding tools such as Claude Code, Codex or equivalent, including working from specifications, reviewing and validating agent-generated changes, and integrating them safely into a shared codebase. Eligibility and willingness to obtain and maintain a national security clearance (e.g. German Ü2/Ü3 or equivalent), as the role involves work for defence and security customers. Nice to Have : Experience with Earth observation data, STAC, COGs, thermal imagery, remote-sensing analytics, GIS or satellite data platforms. Practical geospatial engineering experience with tools such as GeoPandas, rasterio/GDAL, xarray, spatial databases, GIS or geospatial web services. Experience with React/TypeScript or another language/framework that helps you rapidly build interactive analytical tools. Experience with DuckDB and analytical data workflows; semantic search, embeddings, vector databases, knowledge graphs, SPARQL or entity-resolution workflows are also valuable. Experience with agent frameworks or protocols such as LangGraph, pydantic-ai, LangChain or MCP, multi-source data fusion, or taking a prototype through product validation and handing it over to a production engineering team. Why join constellr? High-Impact National Security Mission: Be part of an ambitious mission to launch the world’s first commercial thermal infrared constellation, delivering sovereign-grade "behavioural" intelligence from space to close critical blind spots for defence, intelligence, and maritime surveillance agencies. Fast-Paced "New Space" Innovation: Work at the leading edge of a rapidly evolving defence sector. By leveraging modern, agile New Space methodologies, you will help design, build, and deploy dual-use thermal payloads into operational orbit at a speed and scale that traditional aerospace models simply cannot match. Broad Interdisciplinary Exposure: Accelerate your professional growth by collaborating daily with a diverse, international cohort of astrophysicists, software developers, and defence-system engineers. Inclusive & Scientific Culture: Join a high-trust team deeply committed to diversity, inclusion, and scientific excellence, where academic curiosity is paired with high autonomy and a flat hierarchy. Financial Upside & Well-being: Benefit from a Virtual Share Option Plan (VSOP) for permanent employees, giving you a true stake in the company's growth, alongside highly flexible hybrid working models.
